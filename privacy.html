Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Miniv Clip Publisher is a desktop application used to prepare and publish gaming video content to TikTok.

Information We Access

When a user connects their TikTok account, the application may access information necessary to authenticate the user and publish video content through TikTok's official API.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide the application's video publishing functionality.

Miniv Clip Publisher does not sell personal information to third parties.

Data Storage

Authentication credentials and access tokens may be stored locally on the user's computer when required for the operation of the application.

Third-Party Services

The application uses TikTok services and APIs. TikTok's own privacy policy and terms may also apply.

Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the application developer.
